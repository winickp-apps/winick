The Problem

mBELLAb PS needed proper pipeline visibility. Opportunities were tracked inconsistently, quotes were filed loosely in Word documents, and activity notes lived in people's heads. Across three sister companies sharing founders and staff, compliance obligations like trade licences, visas, and insurance renewals were falling through the cracks.

What Was Built
Pipeline tracking with live KPIs, deadline alerts, and activity logging
Quote letter generation: branded Word doc in under 60 seconds
Cross-entity renewals register (licences, memberships, insurance)
Employee document tracker with passport expiry alerts
Supplier & contractor directories linked to SharePoint
Role-based access: engineers see what they need, admins see everything
ISO quality objectives linked to specific opportunities
Live dashboard with finance tab and recent activity feed
"The technology lowers the barrier to entry dramatically. The strategic lens is what makes it useful. Understanding the business, identifying the friction, scoping the right solution. That judgement is not something AI provides on its own."
The Business

mBELLAb Power Solutions is a specialist engineering and project management firm operating across the UAE, managing high-value tenders and subcontracting work in the power distribution sector. Projects span RTU supply and commissioning, ADMS integration, and infrastructure delivery for clients including TAQA Distribution and Abu Dhabi Municipality.

The business is founder-led, with two principals who also run two sister companies: mBELLAb Business Services & Administration, a management consultancy, and Sentinel Capital Ltd (SENCAP), a DFSA-regulated investment advisory firm based in DIFC, Dubai. Three businesses, two founders, a lean team, and a lot of moving parts.

The Approach

Rather than engaging a development agency or waiting for a budget cycle, the decision was made to build something immediately using AI-assisted development. Working iteratively with Claude (Anthropic), a fully functional web-based operations portal was designed, built, and refined without writing a single line of code manually.

The approach: describe the problem and what good looks like; iterate rapidly on working prototypes; test in the real environment; report what was broken or needed changing; repeat. The result is a production-ready, browser-based application connected to Supabase as its data backend, secured through a Cloudflare Worker proxy, with role-based login. No installation, no app store, no IT department.

What This Demonstrates

Most growing businesses run on operational infrastructure that was never really designed. It accumulated. Spreadsheets became the system of record. Email became the project management tool. Institutional knowledge lives in one or two people's heads. The cost of fixing this used to be high enough that most businesses just lived with it. That's no longer true.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does "fractional" actually mean?+
You get a senior operator embedded in your business, without the cost of a full-time hire. Engagements are typically 2–4 days per week, structured around what the business needs rather than a fixed job description.
How is this different from a management consultant?+
Consultants diagnose and recommend. I diagnose and do. I don't leave a slide deck. I stay until the systems are built and the team is running them. The output is operational change, not a report.
What kind of businesses do you work with?+
Founder-led and owner-managed businesses, typically between AED 1M and AED 20M revenue, that are growing but haven't built the operational infrastructure to support that growth. I've worked across fintech, SaaS, engineering, professional services, and investment management.
Do we need a technical team to use AI-built tools?+
No. Everything I build runs in a browser with no installation required. Your team logs in and uses it. The technical complexity is entirely behind the scenes. Your job is to tell me what you need.
How quickly can you start?+
Usually within 2–3 weeks of our initial conversation, subject to availability. The first step is a diagnostic call, no commitment required, to establish fit and what the right engagement looks like.
What does an engagement cost?+
Engagements are structured as a monthly retainer based on days per week committed. Pricing is discussed directly and depends on scope and duration. The right starting point is a conversation.
